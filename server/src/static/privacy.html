Privacy Policy

PARCOM · Effective 2026-07-27 · Applies to the par-rt-db service at rtdb.pardev.net

PARCOM ("we," "our," or "us") operates the par-rt-db service ("the service"). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Sign-in data. When you sign in with Google or GitHub, the service receives your verified email address and the display name from that account. We never receive or store your account password.
  • Content you create. Documents and files you write to a database are stored in that database. Files uploaded through the storage API are served at public URLs — anyone with a file's URL can fetch it until it is deleted.
  • Authentication credentials. Machine tokens are stored only as one-way hashes; the raw token is shown once when issued and is never persisted in readable form.

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and decide which databases you may access (per-database allowlist).
  • To attribute the data you create to your account (per-row ownership).
  • To keep you signed in via a session cookie in your browser.

We do not use your data for advertising or cross-service tracking.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We share it only as required to operate the service (for example, the provider you authenticate with confirms your identity during sign-in) or as compelled by law. Public storage URLs are shared only to the extent you distribute them.

4. Storage and security

Account and application data is stored in the service's self-hosted database. Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access is gated by authentication and per-database authorization checked on every operation.

5. Retention and deletion

Your sign-in data is kept while your account is active. Data you create persists until you or a database administrator deletes it. You may request deletion of your account data at any time (see contact below).

6. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: probello@gmail.com.
